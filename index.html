The Proof

NVIDIA, Intel, AMD, and Microsoft have each confirmed cross-hardware floating-point divergence is expected behavior at their layer. None owns it. None is fixing it.

The Record

What if my machines match?

          
If your hex view matches one of the results (A, B, or C) above, those machines behaved identically on this test. That does not prove they share identical hardware (as seen by the Intel and AMD results in Result C). To see the divergence at the binary level on identical environments, run the six-node harness in the x86-float32-output-divergence repo.
